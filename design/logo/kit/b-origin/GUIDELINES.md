# AXIS logo guidelines: Origin

## 1. The logo
- **Idea:** Two axes and one plotted point: your notes start from your own origin.
- **Colour logic:** everything is Ink except the point, which is Signal vermilion. That accent is the brand's one moment of colour.
- **Versions:**
  - Horizontal lockup (primary).
  - Stacked lockup.
  - Symbol only (the app icon and favicon).
  - Wordmark only.

  Each comes in colour, reversed (for dark backgrounds), black, white and one-colour Ink.
- **Files:**
  - `axis-b-origin-*.svg`: the masters.
  - `digital/`: one-colour versions, plus PNGs at 64, 512 and 1024 px (1200 px wide for the lockups) and the desktop app icon (`axis-b-origin-app-app-icon.svg` and `-1024.png`).
  - `web/`: `favicon.ico`, `favicon.svg`, the PNG icon set, `site.webmanifest` and `head-snippet.html`.
- **Small sizes:** below 48 px, use `axis-b-origin-symbol-small.svg`. It's a heavier cut with a bigger accent, so it stays clear at 16–32 px.

## 2. Clear space
Keep a clear zone around the logo equal to **the diameter of the signal point in the symbol (56 of the 256 grid)**. The zone scales with the logo.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal lockup | 96 px wide | 25 mm wide |
| Stacked lockup | 48 px wide | 15 mm wide |
| Symbol | 16 px (small cut) | 6 mm |

## 4. Colour
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) | Pantone (nearest; check a swatch book) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | #16181D | 22 24 29 | 24 17 0 89 | Black 6 C |
| Signal vermilion | #E4472B | 228 71 43 | 0 69 81 11 | Warm Red C |
| White | #FFFFFF | 255 255 255 | 0 0 0 0 | — |

**Contrast:**
- Ink on white: 17.8:1.
- Signal on white: 4.0:1.
- Signal on Ink: 4.4:1.

All three meet the 3:1 minimum for graphics. Signal is a colour for accents; don't use it for small body text on white.

**Approved pairs:**
- Colour logo on white or a light surface.
- Reversed logo (white with Signal) on Ink.
- Black on white, and white on black or on photos (on a calm area).
- One-colour Ink wherever only one colour is available, such as engraving, fax or one-colour print.

Never put the colour logo on a Signal background: the accent disappears.

## 5. Typography
- The wordmark is drawn as geometric shapes, not typed. It needs no font licence, and it must never be retyped.
- **Suggested text alongside it:** Inter (SIL Open Font License) for UI and documents, falling back to the system sans-serif. This is an assumption; any neutral grotesque works.

## 6. Don'ts
- Don't stretch, squash or rotate the logo.
- Don't recolour outside the palette, or move the accent to another part of the mark.
- Don't add shadows, outlines, gradients or effects.
- Don't rearrange or resize the parts of a lockup.
- Don't place the logo on busy backgrounds without a container.
- Don't retype the wordmark.

## 7. Notes
- **Favicons:** `favicon.svg` switches to white in dark browser themes. `favicon.ico` and the PNGs are Ink with the accent, which is clearest on light tabs and fainter on dark ones.
- **Trademark:** these designs haven't been through a trademark search. Do a professional clearance search before registering or launching them.
