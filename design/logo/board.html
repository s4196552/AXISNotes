What we heard

The brief

A local-first desktop knowledge base: notes stay as plain Markdown files on your own machine, with links, a graph, grids, canvases, handwriting and optional AI using your own keys. For students, researchers and heavy note-takers.

groundedpreciseconnectedcalmyours

Success: distinct from the category's blue/purple gems, graphs and brains; recognisable at 16 px; works in one colour and reversed; carries the name AXIS or the idea of an axis.
