# T-246 handoff — Settings grouping and theming

## What was built

- **Grouped pages.** The Settings sidebar has 11 pages instead of 16. Three pages hold
  several sections, switched with tabs at the top of the page:
  - **Appearance**: Theme & layout · Colours · Icon & image packs
  - **Commands & shortcuts**: Quick commands · Keyboard shortcuts
  - **AI**: Providers & models · Agent · Claude
  Tabs are a proper tab list (`role="tablist"`, arrow keys move between them, the panel is
  labelled by its tab). Section ids are unchanged, so `<Settings section="claude">` and the
  command palette's section links open the right page and tab. Search still lists every
  section by its own name.
- **App logo once.** It was on both Appearance and Icon & image packs; now that they share
  a page it stays on Theme & layout only.
- **Themed throughout.** AXIS Hub (Plugins page and the Hub in panels) no longer uses fixed
  dark-green colours: pack icons, empty states, dialogs (border, shadow, scrim, inputs,
  errors), notices and the app picker use the theme's tokens. Its danger colour now reads
  the app's `--danger` (it read a token the app never set) and its button text
  `--accent-fg`.
- **Liquid glass Settings.** Under Translucent → Liquid glass the Settings sidebar is a glass
  pane with capsule rows, and the new tabs are glass capsules, like the app's sidebars.
