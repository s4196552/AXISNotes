## What was built
`e2e/specs/structured.e2e.ts` (6 tests) and a `structured` vault seed in `wdio.conf.ts`. The tests cover:
- **Grid**: editing cells and a formula-bar formula, saving to disk, and reopening with identical values and no rewrite (the mtime is unchanged).
- **Canvas**: adding a note card from the picker and a freehand stroke (pen tool plus pointer drag), both saved to `.axcanvas`. After reopening, the card and stroke are still there, and the card shows an edit made to the note on disk.
- **Tasks**: completing an overdue task from the tasks view, which writes `[x]` and a `✅` date to the file.
- **Computed properties and time**: `total` = 705, and a timer start/stop leaves a closed `time_log` entry that the time report lists.

QA screenshots are in `.agents/qa/phase-3/` (gitignored).
