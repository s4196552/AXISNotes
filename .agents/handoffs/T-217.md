## Included behaviour

**Teach the AI** is available in Skills. The user teaches a curious AI student that asks one relevant question at a time, tries paraphrasing or applying explanations, and offers specific feedback on wrap-up. It reuses selected providers/models, source restrictions, multi-turn history and automatically saved Markdown/HTML transcripts. Notes are reference material, not a lesson the user has already given. See the T-216 handoff/review for implementation details and limitations.
