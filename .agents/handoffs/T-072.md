# T-072 handoff: Translucent theme effects (Clear, Blur, Frosted, Mica, Mica Alt)

**Built by:** Claude. **Branch:** `agent/claude/T-072` (from `release/0.3.1` at 292ba18). **Requested by:** the human ("for transparency add a effect slider, like frosted, translucent and look for others").

## What was built
- **Settings → Appearance (while Translucent is the theme):** the "Blur behind the window" checkbox becomes **Effect**.
  - It's a stepped range input with `aria-valuetext` set to the effect's name, and clickable names underneath.
  - The stops are Clear, Blur, Frosted, Mica and Mica Alt. The hint describes the chosen one, or, when it needs Windows' transparency effects and they're off, says where to turn them on.
  - The slider stays usable then, so the choice is ready for when the setting is turned on.
- **`src/app/appearance.ts`:**
  - `WINDOW_EFFECTS` lists each effect's label, note and Tauri material: `null`, `blur`, `acrylic`, `mica`, `tabbed`.
  - `windowEffectInfo(id)` returns an effect; anything unknown gets Frosted.
  - `transparencyEffectsOff` and `onTransparencyEffectsChange` are renamed from the blur-specific names.
  - `applyTranslucency` sends the material, or `null`, and skips repeats.
- **Config:** `appearance.windowEffect` (default `"frosted"`) replaces `windowBlur`. `migrateWindowEffect` turns a 0.3.1 `windowBlur: false` into `"clear"`.
- **Backend:** `setWindowBlur(on)` becomes `setWindowEffect(effect | null)`, which calls Tauri's `setEffects` or `clearEffects`. It needs no new permission; `allow-set-effects` covers both.

## What the effects look like, measured
On this machine Windows' Transparency effects are off (`EnableTransparency = 0`, and WebView2 reports `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`). I captured a debug build over a test pattern window placed directly behind it, at 40% opacity:
- **Clear:** the pattern shows through.
- **Blur, Acrylic, Mica and Tabbed:** Windows accepts every call (`set_effects` returns ok), but each paints a solid dark fill (19, 19, 21).

So the app leaves effects off while the setting is off, and the window looks Clear. **The look of each effect with Transparency effects on couldn't be checked here**, because that's the user's system setting.

## Review round 1 (Codex: changes-requested)
- **Finding 2 (medium), fixed:** switching materials didn't clear the previous one. I confirmed this in Tauri 2.11.6's `vibrancy/windows.rs`: `apply_effects` applies on top, and blur/acrylic (accent policy) and Mica (DWM backdrop) are separate mechanisms. It also discards errors, which is why the IPC call always reports ok.
  - `setWindowEffect` now always calls `clearEffects()` before `setEffects`.
  - `applyTranslucency` runs changes one at a time through a queue, so dragging across the stops can't interleave calls.
  - Tests that fail without the fix: `tauriBackend.test.ts` (clear before every set) and "sends effect changes one at a time, in order".
- **Finding 1 (high), not in this task:** "Install and restart" can lose unsaved edits. That's T-066's updater code (`src/features/updates/updates.ts`), which reaches this diff only because `release/0.3.1` isn't merged into `main`. It's proposed as its own follow-up task, outside T-072's file ownership.

## How to test
- **Settings and appearance tests:** `pnpm exec vitest run src/features/settings src/app`. Themes tests cover:
  - the material sent for each effect, clamping, and no effect when the window is solid;
  - an unknown saved id falling back, repeats not resent, and other themes sending none;
  - effects being skipped and the hint shown while Windows' setting is off, the hint following the chosen effect (none for Clear), and the hint clearing live when the setting is turned on;
  - the slider and the clickable names saving `windowEffect`;
  - the 0.3.1 migration;
  - controls disabled where the window can't be see-through.
- **E2E (`knowledge.e2e.ts`):** steps the slider through every stop and checks each is saved, with no warnings. On Windows it also calls the window with each material and `null`, and expects every call to succeed.
- **Native Windows WebView2 runs:**
  - `knowledge.e2e.ts` passed, with all four materials accepted.
  - The full suite passes: 9 of 9 spec files, 58 tests.
  - A WebDriver screenshot of Settings showed the five names lined up under the stops. Frosted was highlighted at the thumb, and the "needs Windows' transparency effects" hint appeared, because they're off on this machine.
- **Other checks:** `pnpm test` passes 343 tests. `pnpm lint` and `pnpm typecheck` are clean, and Prettier is clean on the changed files.
