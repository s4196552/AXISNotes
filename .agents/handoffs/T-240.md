# T-240 — AXISNotes 0.3.90 Windows update

- **Emoji and icons** — `:` opens the picker immediately. It includes emoji,
  built-in icons, and vault-pack icons. **Add icon…** imports an image into
  the vault's **My icons** pack for reuse in notes.
- **Copy code** — Copy a fenced block from its top-right button in live
  preview or while editing. Indentation and blank lines are preserved.
- **Markdown line controls** — Use **+** to add a line and open the command
  menu. Drag the grip to move a line or block, or double-click it to select.
- **Mermaid previews** — Diagrams render without a border. Click the graphic
  to edit its source. Resizing still saves the width in the note.
- **Quote template** — **Ctrl+/** → **Quote** inserts a callout with
  `TITLE` and selects the `Contents` placeholder.
