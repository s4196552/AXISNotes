# T-037 handoff: The AXIS logo

**Built by:** Claude. **Branch:** `agent/claude/T-037`

## What was built
- The user's logo (`Templogo.svg`, three rounded strokes in red, blue and green) is now the app logo.
  - It was cleaned of its embedded content-credential metadata and its white background square: 21 KB down to 2 KB.
  - The master is `src/assets/logo.svg`.
- All desktop icons were regenerated with `pnpm tauri icon src/assets/logo.svg`. The extension icons follow via `pnpm build:extension`.
- In the app, the logo appears on the welcome screen, in Settings → About (the `Logo` component in `src/app/Logo.tsx`) and as the favicon (`index.html`).
- Installers were built: `AXIS_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe` and `AXIS_0.1.0_x64_en-US.msi`.

## How to test
- `pnpm test`: 264 passing.
- Install with the setup exe; the Start menu entry, taskbar and window all show the new icon.
