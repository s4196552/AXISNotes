# T-039 handoff: Big and messy folders

**Built by:** Claude. **Branch:** `agent/claude/T-039`

## What was built
- **`vault::Walk`:** a tree and index walk that never fails. It skips unreadable and broken items, follows folder links once and skips link loops. It's used by `list_tree` and `collect_notes`.
- **Opening without waiting:** `Index::open_unsynced` plus a background `sync_with_progress` (batches of 500, a busy timeout, cancelled when another vault opens). Progress goes out on the `index://progress` event, and the status bar shows "Indexing notes… n of N".
- **Tests:**
  - Rust: a broken link plus a loop, a link to a folder outside the vault, sync progress and cancel.
  - E2E: the `vault` seed now has a looping link and a broken link.
  - Probe: `AXISNOTES_PROBE=<dir> cargo test --release probe_big_vault -- --ignored --nocapture`.

## Measured
On a test folder with 20k notes, 40k other files, a 50 MB binary, a loop, a broken link and a locked folder:
- The tree loads in 0.31 s; before, it failed.
- The index builds in 5.6 s in the background; before, it took 24 s in the foreground.
- Reopening takes 0.3 s.

## Known gaps
- Every external change reloads the whole tree.
- The file tree isn't virtualized for single folders with tens of thousands of files.
