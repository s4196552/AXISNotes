# T-284 handoff: faster, steadier tests

## What was built

- `src/test/setup.ts` no longer loads formula functions. `src/lib/computed.test.ts` and `src/lib/formula/formula.test.ts`, the only files that evaluate formulas, load them in `beforeAll`. The local-storage cleanup skips files that run in Node.
- 28 `*.test.ts` files that never touch the DOM start with `// @vitest-environment node`. `src/lib/diagram.test.ts` needs Mermaid's DOM and stays on jsdom.
- Lint warnings, all fixed by moving non-component exports out of component files:
  - Study cards: sessions and results go to `studyBlocks/session.ts`, mounting to `studyBlocks/mountCard.ts` (with `createElement`), and `KIND_LABEL` to `model.ts`. The cards now export only their views, and the `studyBlocks.ts` loaders pair each view with `mountCard` while still loading lazily.
  - Ribbon: `useRibbon` and `useRibbonDrag` move to `ribbon/useRibbon.ts`.
  - AI: `noteAccess` moves to `ai/noteAccess.ts`.
  - Page embeds: `mountPageEmbed` moves to `drafts/mountPageEmbed.ts`, and `pageHeight` to `drafts/pageHeight.ts`. `embeds.ts` loads `mountPageEmbed`.

## Results

| Run | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Default `pnpm test` | 60–236 s, with timeouts | 1282 tests in 54 s, no timeouts |
| `--maxWorkers=4` | 92–97 s | 81 s |

`eslint .` reports no problems.

## Known gaps

- Short real sleeps are still there: `FileTree.test.tsx:127` (550 ms), `layout.test.tsx:179` (250 ms), `sidebarLayout.test.tsx:374` (120 ms). They total under a second, and fake timers would fight the async setup.
- No Vitest `projects` split or `isolate: false`. Per-file environment comments give most of the gain at lower risk.
