# T-138 - Fold arrow styles

Built by Claude on agent/claude/T-138 from main a57c91a, at the human's request ("Give me options to change the arrow icon/s", with a screenshot of the heading fold arrows).

## Behaviour

- Settings → Appearance → Fold arrows offers six styles as tiles, each previewed closed and open: Triangle (default, as before), Chevron, Arrow, Circled chevron, Plus and minus, Boxed plus and minus.
- The choice applies at once to every heading and toggle-list arrow, in every theme, and is saved as `appearance.foldArrow` in `.axisnotes/config.json`. An unknown value falls back to Triangle.
- Arrow styles turn 90° when opened; plus/minus styles swap icon instead of turning.

## How to test

`pnpm vitest run src/features/settings/appearance.test.tsx`. In the app: open a note with headings, Settings → Appearance → Fold arrows, pick a style; the arrows change immediately and the style survives a restart.
