# T-197 handoff: Ask your vault, with citations

## What was built
- "Ask your vault" (command palette, Ctrl+Shift+J) opens Ask AI in vault mode. Each question finds the best-matching heading sections across the vault with the full-text index, within 8k tokens.
- The Rust layer reads and rule-checks every section itself.
- The answer cites `[[note#Heading]]`. A Sources list links every passage sent, and citations to anything else are struck through.
- "Include local-only notes" (off by default) brings in local-only notes, and then only a local model can answer.
- `RunRequest.atLeast` holds follow-ups (Ask AI and AI blocks) to the strictest rule of the conversation's earlier answers.
- Attached notes and passages now come right after the system prompt, for prompt caching.

## Files
- Rust: `index/passages.rs` (new), `index/{search,mod}.rs`, `ai/service.rs` (+ tests), `commands.rs` (`vault_passages`), `lib.rs`.
- TypeScript: `features/ai/{vaultAsk.ts,vaultAsk.test.tsx,AskAi.tsx,AiBlockView.tsx,aiBlockStore.ts,AnswerText.tsx,ai.css}`, `features/tasks/InlineText.tsx` (optional `isKnown`), `features/commands/{registry,ui,Modals}`, `ipc/{types,tauriBackend,memoryBackend,memoryAi}`.

## How to test
- `cargo test -- passages ai::service`
- `pnpm exec vitest run src/features/ai/vaultAsk.test.tsx`
- In the app: Ctrl+Shift+J, ask about something in your notes. "Using N passages" lists what was sent, and the Sources links open the notes.

## Known gaps
- Keyword retrieval misses notes that use different words; a semantic mode (embeddings) is a separate future task.
- The passage budget (8k tokens) and per-note limit (3 sections) are fixed.
