# T-087: Tab groups, like Chrome's

**Goal:** tabs can be selected together and gathered into named, coloured groups that
collapse and move as one.

**Requirements:**
- **Selecting:** Ctrl/Cmd+click adds or removes a tab from the selection; Shift+click
  selects from the current tab to the clicked one. A plain click (or Escape) clears it. The
  tab menu acts on the whole selection when it's opened on a selected tab.
- **Grouping:** the tab menu offers Add to new group, Add to an existing group and Remove
  from group. A new group gets the next free colour and opens its editor.
- **Group chip:** before the group's tabs. An unnamed group shows a coloured circle; a
  named one shows its name on a coloured pill. Clicking the chip collapses or expands the
  group. Right-click opens the editor: name, colour (9), New tab in group, Ungroup, Close
  group. Grouped tabs carry the group's colour.
- **Collapsing:** hides the group's tabs. If the active tab is in it, the nearest tab
  outside becomes active (or a new tab if there's none). Opening a hidden tab any other
  way (Ctrl+1, quick switcher, …) expands its group. Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+1–8 skip hidden
  tabs.
- **Moving:** drag the chip to move the whole group (it never lands inside another group).
  Dragging a tab next to a group's tab puts it in that group; dropping it elsewhere takes it
  out. A group's tabs always stay together.
- **New tabs** opened next to a grouped tab join its group.
- **Persistence:** groups (name, colour, collapsed) are saved with the tabs.
