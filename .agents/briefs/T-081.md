# T-081: Resizable side panels with a sliding animation

**Goal:** the left and right side panels can be resized, and panels slide smoothly but
quickly when shown, hidden or snapped to a new width.

**Requirements:**
- A drag handle on each panel's inner edge; the drag follows the pointer exactly (no lag).
- Widths are per-device (local storage, beside show/hide), clamped to 180–600 px, and the
  note area keeps at least 320 px when a drag starts.
- Keyboard: the handle is a focusable `separator`; arrows ±16 px, Shift ±64, Home/End.
  Double-click resets the default (260 / 280).
- Show/hide, keyboard steps and the reset slide over ~180 ms with an ease-out curve. A
  closing panel is hidden from assistive technology and inert at once; the right panel
  (live graph) unmounts once it has slid away. The slide plays even under reduced motion
  (the user asked for it with Windows animations off).
- Hiding a panel mid-drag ends the drag cleanly.

**Files:** `src/app/layout.ts`, `src/app/layout.test.tsx`, `src/App.tsx`,
`src/features/panels/{PanelResizer.tsx,presence.ts,Sidebars.tsx,panels.css}`,
`src/styles/app.css` (shell and sidebar-slot rules only), `.agents/**` for T-081.

**Tests:** `pnpm lint && pnpm typecheck && pnpm test`; drag, keyboard, reset, persistence and
