# T-274: AI notes, Summary and Tutor skills

The human asked (2026-11-23): "Add skills for AI notes, Summary and Tutor w/ html and markdown support".

## Scope

`src/features/skills/{builtinSkills,skillGroups,skillIcons}.ts`, `src/features/editor/quickCommands.ts` (section icon), their tests, `e2e/specs/skills.e2e.ts` and task coordination.

## Behaviour

- Add three built-in skills as SKILL.md text, so they can also be copied into the vault and edited:
  - **AI notes** (`ai-notes`, default Markdown): rewrite source material as organized study notes, keeping the source's facts and terms, flagging gaps instead of inventing, with key terms and check questions about the subject.
  - **Summary** (`summary`, default Markdown): gist, key points, and any decisions, open questions or actions, only from the notes.
  - **Tutor** (`tutor`, chat): a one-to-one tutor that checks what the learner knows, explains one idea at a time, hints before solving and wraps up on request.
- Each has a "## Format: Markdown" and "## Format: HTML" section (Tutor, like the other conversations, is kept as a Markdown note or HTML transcript).
- A new "Notes & summaries" menu category holds AI notes and Summary; Tutor goes first under "Learn & understand". Distinct icons.
- Reuse the existing provider, permissions, result-saving and format-switch paths. No new dependencies.
