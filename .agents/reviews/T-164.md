# T-164 review: split panes with draggable tabs

**Verdict: approve** (Claude self-review, 2026-11-05)

## What changed

- `src/app/panes.ts` (new) holds the pure workspace model: a tree of row/column splits, each pane with its own `TabsState`. It covers:
  - moving a tab between panes, or dropping one on a pane's middle or edge;
  - Split right/down, closing panes, and resizing a divider;
  - layout boxes and touched edges, the drop zone under the pointer, and saving per vault.
  - The tree normalizes itself: a split with one child collapses, and a split inside a same-direction split is flattened into it.
- `src/app/store.ts`: `tabs` remains the focused pane's tabs, so every existing tab action, shortcut and caller works on the focused pane unchanged. The store also gains:
  - `panes` and the actions `focusPane`, `moveTabToPane`, `dropTabOnPane`, `splitPane`, `closePane` and `resizeSplit`;
  - renames, trashes and the restore-time prune applied to every pane;
  - closing a pane once its last tab closes, when it isn't the only pane;
  - saving `axisnotes.panes:<root>` while still saving `axisnotes.tabs:<root>`, so an older build reopens with the focused pane's tabs.
- `src/features/panes/Workspace.tsx` (new) positions panes absolutely from the tree, so splitting or closing a pane never remounts the others' editors. It also provides:
  - resizable dividers (double-click evens them out);
  - a drop-zone highlight during a drag;
  - a press or focus inside a pane making it the focused pane;
  - one view element per pane, so focusing a pane doesn't remount its note.
- `src/features/tabs/TabBar.tsx`: renders one pane's row. Its drag target can be:
  - its own row (same behaviour as before);
  - another pane's row, at a position, joining a group next to it;
  - a pane's content, either the middle or an edge.
  - The drop marker is shared through `tabDrop.ts`, so the pane under the pointer draws it.
  - The tab menu gains Split right and Split down.
  - Only the top row is a window drag region.
- `NavButtons.tsx`: it shows its own pane's history. Only the focused pane listens for the mouse side buttons; before, each pane would have fired Back once.
- `Editor.tsx` / `activeEditor.ts`: only the focused pane's editor is the command target and shows its word count. Unmounting only clears its own view. When the same note is open in two panes, a save in one reloads the other, or shows the existing "changed on disk" banner if it has unsaved edits.
- Other changes:
  - Command palette: Split right, Split down and Close pane.
  - Zen mode: any pane's tab row keeps the top revealed.
  - CSS: the corner rows keep their space for the panel and window buttons.

## Findings

No blocking findings. Notes:

1. Graph, Tasks and Time views in a pane that isn't focused still read the store's focused note (`activePath`) for their "back to note" button and local context. This only matters while two such views are side by side.
2. Tabs in an unfocused pane use `aria-controls` pointing at the focused pane's `#tab-panel`, which is minor for accessibility.
3. Moving several selected tabs between panes at once is not supported; one tab moves at a time. Groups move within their own row only.
4. Bug found while checking in the preview and fixed: when a drop closed the source pane, its drop marker was left on screen. It is now cleared on drop and on unmount, and a test covers it.

## Tests

- `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck` and the Prettier check on the changed files passed. The full `pnpm test` result is recorded below. No Rust changes.
- New `src/app/panes.test.ts` (10 cases) covers:
  - edge splits, middle moves, and refusing to split a pane off its own only tab;
  - siblings in the same split, moving into another row and closing the emptied pane;
  - closing panes, divider limits, layout edges and dividers;
  - drop zones, save/restore and invalid saves;
  - every-pane renames.
- New `src/features/panes/Workspace.test.tsx` (4 cases) covers:
  - a pointer drag to the right edge splitting, and a drag to the other row joining and closing the empty pane, with no marker left;
  - a press focusing a pane;
  - Split down from the tab menu, and closing the last tab closing the pane;
  - the same note open in two panes staying in step, and the command target following focus;
  - the layout surviving a vault switch.
- `src/test/setup.ts` also clears `axisnotes.panes:` keys between tests.
- Preview (vite, demo vault, 1400×820):
  - dragging the Biology tab to the right edge split the area side by side;
  - a pointer-event drag of Welcome to the bottom of the right pane stacked them, and the emptied left pane closed;
  - the layout survived a reload, and dragging the divider resized it to 30/70;
  - dragging Welcome back into the top row merged everything into one pane.
