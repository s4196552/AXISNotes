# T-265 review: compact heading list, left or right side

**Verdict:** approve (Claude, self-verified under the 2026-11-10 rule)

## What the human asked

With a screenshot of a compact heading list beside a note: "Make the window smaller to
something like this" and "Allow the user to choose to have it in the left or right option in
settings".

## What changed

- `headingIndicator.ts`: `headingIndicator(side)` returns one cached extension per side
  (default right). `offset()` measures the free margin on the chosen side. On the left the
  free room is the `LEFT_ROOM` strip only, because the content's start padding already holds
  the + / grip controls and fold arrows. `revealActive()` scrolls the list to the current
  section when the pointer enters. Depth indent is 10px instead of 12px.
- `editor.css`: the list is 210px wide, at most min(60vh, 480px) tall, 12px text with
  3px × 8px items. Long headings wrap instead of being cut off with an ellipsis. It has a
  thin scrollbar and contained overscroll, and it opens towards the text on either side.
  `.cm-heading-side-left` widens the note's start padding by 28px (`LEFT_ROOM`) so the rail
  never sits under the line controls.
- `config.ts`: `appearance.headingIndicatorSide: "right" | "left"`, default `"right"`.
  Existing configs merge to right.
- `Editor.tsx`: the compartment reconfigures when the indicator is turned on or off or its
  side changes.
- `Settings.tsx`: new "Heading indicator side" select (Right / Left), disabled while the
  indicator is off. The indicator hint now says "edge" instead of "right edge".
