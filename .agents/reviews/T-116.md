# Review: T-116 — Small windows and phone widths: side panels open over the note

**Verdict:** approve (self-review by Claude, the lead, per the 2026-10-17 agreement)

Finding L1 of the 2026-10-20 UX review, built to mockup M5 option A (drawers, the recommended one; the human picked batch 3 without choosing another). Before this the CSS had no width-based layout: at 390 px the note disappeared behind the two sidebars, and at 1024 px both stayed docked. Reviewed `git diff main...agent/claude/T-116` in full.

## What changed

- **Compact mode below 1100 px** (`COMPACT_QUERY` in `app/layout.ts`, DESIGN.md §3):
  - the note takes the whole width, and the side panels open over it one at a time with a scrim;
  - the panel toggles in the tab row, the Ctrl+\\ and left/right panel commands, search and "Reveal open file" all open the overlay instead of docking;
  - the overlay closes on the scrim, on Escape (unless a dialog is open) and when a file or view opens from it;
  - overlays are capped at 86% of the width, slide in and out (instant with reduced motion), and the resize handles are hidden.
- **Layout state:** the docked layout (left/right, widths) is kept and saved as before. Compact mode and the open overlay aren't saved, and crossing the breakpoint closes the overlay. `isPanelOpen` gives the toggle, App and tests one answer.
- **Phones (below 700 px):** less padding around the note title and text, and dialogs nearer the top. Settings puts its sections in a horizontally scrolling row above the content, with fields stacked.

## Findings (most severe first)

1. **Minor, accepted:** no bottom tab bar. That was option B; the drawers reuse the overlay code. The future native mobile clients can revisit it.
2. **Minor:** checked in the browser preview at 1024×700 and 390×844, not on a real phone or touch device. In the desktop app, a window narrower than 1100 px (a half-screen window on a 1920 px display) is now compact. The default window is 1280 px, so it opens docked as before.
3. **Minor, accepted:** the grid toolbar and canvas tools aren't re-laid out for phones (they scroll or wrap as before).
