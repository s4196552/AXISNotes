## What it fixes

The release-build check of 0.3.97 found the heading indicator (T-254) hidden in the real app
whenever both side panels were open.

- **Cause:** it measured its room from CodeMirror's content box, which spans the whole
  editor. The text column is narrower (the editor width setting), so the true margin
  between the text and the scrollbar is about 24 px, and the old check always saw almost
  none.
- **Fix:** room is now measured from the text lines (`.cm-line`) to the scrollbar.
- The lines are slightly shorter (16/12/9/6/4 px) and the column is centred in that margin
  (`placement`, `src/features/editor/headingIndicator.ts`).
- It hides only below 20 px.
