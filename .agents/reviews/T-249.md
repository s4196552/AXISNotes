## What it adds

- **High contrast dark / High contrast light** built-in themes after VS Code's High Contrast
  Dark and Light: black (#000) with #6FC3DF contrast borders and #F38518 active/focus
  borders; white with #0F4A85 borders and #006BBD focus; text, links and selection at full
  strength. Panes and dialogs are outlined, the selected file/tab/settings item gets a dashed
  active outline, keyboard focus a solid one, and links are underlined.
- **E-ink** theme: black ink on paper white, greys only (images, video and canvases are
  greyscale), no shadows or glass, and no animations or transitions anywhere.
- **Night light**: a warm multiply filter over the whole window (`#axis-night-light`,
  `pointer-events: none`), with a Warmth slider (10–80 %), working with any theme.
- Settings → Appearance → Theme & layout gets an **Easy on the eyes** section (Night light,
  Warmth, E-ink on/off); the three themes are also tiles in the theme grid.
- The theme removed in 0.3.58, `axis:contrast`, now opens as High contrast dark instead of Dark.
