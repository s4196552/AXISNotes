## What it adds

- **Heading indicator** (`src/features/editor/headingIndicator.ts`, a CodeMirror view plugin):
  a column of short lines at a note's right edge, one per Markdown heading.
  - **Line lengths:** the note's biggest heading level gets the longest line (20 px), whether
    that level is `#` or `####`. Each smaller level is shorter: 15, 11, 8, then 6 px.
  - **Current section:** the section at the top of the view is highlighted while scrolling.
  - **List:** pointing at the lines, or tabbing in, shows the headings as an indented list
    with the current one in the accent colour. Picking one scrolls there and puts the cursor
    on it.
  - **Hidden** when a note has fewer than two headings, or there's under 56 px beside the
    text.
  - The note never changes.
- **Setting:** Settings → Appearance → Theme & layout → **Heading indicator**
  (`appearance.headingIndicator`, on by default). It is applied through a compartment, so
  turning it off removes the plugin and its scroll listener.
