# T-117 review: Liquid glass window effect

**Verdict: approve** (Claude self-review under the 2026-10-17 agreement)

## What it does

Translucent theme → Effect gains a **Liquid glass** stop (between Frosted and Mica), modelled on Apple's Liquid Glass (macOS 26):

- Windows draws acrylic behind the see-through window, as for Frosted.
- Inside the window, the sidebars and note pane float inset from the window edges with 18 px corners, a soft shadow and a 1 px gradient rim, brightest at the top-left and bottom-right.
- Tabs, the Files/Search/Tags segment and primary buttons are capsules. The active tab and segment are raised glass controls.
- File-tree rows are rounded pills. The accent colour is Apple's system blue (#007aff / #0a84ff).
- Menus, the command palette, dialogs and CodeMirror autocomplete are clear glass. They blur, saturate and brighten what's behind them, and an SVG displacement filter (`#axis-liquid-lens`, added once by `appearance.ts`) bends the backdrop near their edges like a lens rim.
- With Windows' transparency effects off (`prefers-reduced-transparency`), the glass turns near-solid and the lens is dropped, as Apple's Reduce Transparency does.
- The window buttons move into the floating title bar's corner. In compact windows, an overlay panel frosts the note it covers.

## Findings

No blocking findings.

1. `src/styles/themes.css` (Liquid glass section): the panes never get `backdrop-filter`, mask or `z-index`. The frost layer on dialogs and compact panels is a `::before` at `z-index: -1`, and the rim is an `::after`. This avoids the containing-block and stacking problems that the Glass theme's comment describes for fixed menus. Menus are filtered directly because they are fixed and hold nothing fixed.
2. `src/app/appearance.ts` `windowEffectInfo`: the unknown-value fallback now looks up Frosted by id instead of by index 2, so inserting a stop can't change the default.
3. Low: the 8 px strip above the floating title bar isn't a drag region. Tauri drag regions come from an attribute, not from CSS. The tab row is still draggable.
4. Low: the in-browser preview has an opaque backdrop, so the light-mode rims are faint there. On a see-through window they sit over the desktop.
