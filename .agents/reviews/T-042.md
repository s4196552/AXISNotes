### Checked, no defects

- **Identity (`src/suite/references.ts`):**
  - The key is a JSON tuple, so IDs containing punctuation can't collide. IDs are case-sensitive and must be non-empty after trimming.
  - An unknown `appId` is rejected.
  - Extra locator fields such as `title` or `rowId` are ignored, so identity survives renames and index rebuilds.
  - `resourceKey` throws on invalid input, so `sameResource` can never match malformed refs.
- **AI evaluator (`src/suite/policy.ts:51-85`):**
  - These all deny:
    - an empty policy list;
    - an unknown operation;
    - a malformed candidate;
    - unverified execution or an `unknown` location;
    - an invalid policy, a policy with `unavailable` rules or a Never policy;
    - a non-v1 policy version;
    - a sparse or malformed list, checked through `Array.from`.
  - It checks every policy in turn and stops at the first one that fails, so the result is the intersection of all policies whatever their order. A later invalid policy can't be skipped by an earlier allow.
  - Model matching compares provider, endpoint and model exactly.
  - Every operation requires `read`, and `apply` must be listed explicitly.
  - `private-server` is kept separate from `on-device`.
