## What it adds

Settings → Appearance → **Cursor** tab for the text cursor (caret):

- **Shape:** Bar (default), Block or Underline (`data-caret-shape` on the document, rules in
  `editor.css`).
- **Thickness:** 1–8 px (`--caret-width`; 1 keeps CodeMirror's thin 1.2 px bar). Disabled for
  Block, which is a character wide.
- **Colour:** a colour picker (`--caret-color`) or the theme accent; it also colours the caret
  of every text box (`caret-color` on the document).
- **Blink:** off gives a steady cursor (`data-caret-blink="off"` stops CodeMirror's blink).
- A text box on the tab to try it.

Saved per vault as `appearance.cursor` (`{ shape, width, color, blink }`), applied by
`applyCursor` (`src/app/appearance.ts`).

## Findings

No open findings. Notes:

- Only `#rgb`/`#rrggbb`/`#rrggbbaa` colours reach CSS, so a hand-edited config can't inject
  other CSS; unknown shapes fall back to Bar and thickness is clamped.
- The native caret in text boxes follows the colour only (browsers don't let pages change its
  thickness or shape).
