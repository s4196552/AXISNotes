## What changed

- The row under the vault name has two groups:
  - **Files / Search / Tags:** a segmented control (`role="tablist"`, "Sidebar panels") with the selected panel highlighted;
  - **Home / Graph / Tasks / Time:** plain buttons after a divider (`role="toolbar"`, "Open a view"). They open a tab in the main area and never look selected, so opening Home no longer shows two highlighted icons.
- **Fixes:** the four view buttons sat inside the tab list (wrong for screen readers) and each had `margin-left: auto`, which spread them across the row. Their tooltips now include the Home shortcut.
- **Narrow panels:** a container query tightens the buttons below 250 px, and below 220 px the view buttons move to a second line, so nothing overflows down to the 180 px minimum.
