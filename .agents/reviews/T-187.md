# T-187 review: Boxy layout option

**Verdict: approve** (Claude self-review, 2026-11-07)

## Why

The user asked for "an option to have a boxier UI", with a screenshot of the Claude desktop app as reference: "Don't change the texts and icon format, just the boxy." In the reference, the side panels and the main area are flat rectangles meeting edge to edge, split by thin lines. AXISNotes floats its panes as rounded cards (18 px corners) with 8 px gaps and an 8 px inset from the window edge.

## What changed

- `src/app/config.ts`: `appearance.boxy` (default `false`). Old configs load with it off, through `mergeConfig`.
- `src/app/layout.ts`:
  - `BOXY_PANE_LAYOUT` (inset, gap and corner radius all 0) and `paneLayout(boxy)`.
  - `maxWidth` uses the current layout, read from `data-boxy` on the root, so with Boxy on a panel can grow into the space the gaps used to take.
- `src/App.tsx`:
  - The shell's `--pane-inset`, `--pane-gap` and `--pane-radius` come from `paneLayout(boxy)`.
  - The shell gets the `boxy` class, and the root gets `data-boxy`.
- `src/styles/app.css`, `.shell.boxy`:
  - The panes lose their own borders and corners.
  - The left panel gets a right border and the right panel a left border, so a single line separates each pair.
  - In a narrow window or Zen mode, where panels open over the note, a panel keeps its full border so it still has an edge.
  - The status bar already had its top line.
  - Nothing else changes: text, icons, buttons, chips, menus, dialogs and the editor keep their look.
- `Settings → Appearance`: a **Boxy layout** checkbox with a hint.

## Findings

None.

## Tests

- `src/app/layout.test.tsx`, "boxy layout puts the panels edge to edge with square corners, and back":
  - The shell class and the root's `data-boxy` follow the setting.
  - Inset, gap and radius are 0 px.
  - The left panel's maximum width grows by the 32 px of insets and gaps.
  - Turning it off restores 18 px corners.
- Layout file: 16 passed. Settings tests: pass.
- **Preview** at 1200×760:
  - Normal: rounded floating panels with gaps.
  - Boxy on: panels flush to the window edges and each other, with square corners and single divider lines. The text and icons are the same.
  - Settings → Appearance → Boxy layout toggles it off and on live.
- Full checks: `pnpm lint` and `pnpm typecheck` pass. `vitest run --maxWorkers=3`: 103 files, 926 tests passed. No Rust changes.
