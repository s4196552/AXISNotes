# Review: T-157 Callouts drawn and folded like Obsidian's

**Verdict: approve**

Follows T-156. The user sent Obsidian screenshots of the reference note. Headings, lists, numbers and tasks already matched AXIS at the same line pitches. The callouts did not: AXIS showed a raw `[!NOTE]-` with a left bar, and `+`/`-` did not fold.

## What changed

- `src/features/editor/obsidianSyntax.ts`:
  - Callouts are drawn as Obsidian's box: a tinted rounded background, 12px/24px padding, and a title line in the type's colour (weight 600, line height 1.3).
  - The inside has a paragraph's space above and below (one note-font-size), as Obsidian's `<p>` margins do.
  - Off the title line, `[!type]±` is replaced by the type's Lucide icon. An untitled callout shows the type's name ("Faq", "Warning").
  - `calloutKind` now knows Obsidian's full set of types and aliases: note, abstract/summary/tldr, info, todo, tip/hint/important, success/check/done, question/help/faq, warning/caution/attention, failure/fail/missing, danger/error, bug, example, quote/cite. Unknown types look like a note.
- `src/features/editor/toggles.ts`:
  - Callouts with `+`/`-` fold through the same state field as toggles, flipping the character in the note.
  - A chevron after the title turns when closed. It is keyboard-accessible and labelled "Collapse callout"/"Expand callout".
  - While closed, the title line is the whole box (`cm-callout-closed`).
- `src/features/editor/editor.css` and `src/styles/theme.css`: callout box styles and Obsidian's callout colours as `--callout-*` tokens (`light-dark()`).

## Measured against the user's screenshot (Obsidian at 1.2× zoom, converted to CSS px)

The roughly 1px difference is the title's line box: Segoe UI's bold content area is 21.8px at line height 1.3, and the same rounding appears in Obsidian's own figures. Light and dark themes were checked in the preview.

## Findings

No blocking findings.

1. Minor, `src/features/editor/obsidianSyntax.ts`: like the rest of the live preview, a callout's Markdown shows only on the line being edited. Obsidian switches the whole callout to source while the cursor is anywhere inside it. Keeping AXIS's per-line behaviour avoids the box jumping as the cursor moves.
2. Note: an empty `>` line inside a callout is a full line (24px). Obsidian collapses paragraph margins to 16px.
