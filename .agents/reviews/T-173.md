# T-173 review: Quick Chat, an icon under the Agent

**Verdict: approve** (Claude self-review, 2026-11-06)

## What changed

- A round "Ask the Agent" icon (speech bubble with a plus) sits under the Agent, in its own window and in the browser's in-page Agent. Clicking it opens Quick Chat.
  - **Panel:** a prompt (Enter sends, Shift+Enter adds a line, Escape closes) and a "Reads: <open note>" chip that leaves the note out when clicked.
  - **While running:** the question, then the streamed answer with Stop.
  - **When finished:** Open as draft (focuses AXISNotes and opens the answer as a T-174 draft) and Copy.
- `src/features/agent/agentChat.ts`: Quick Chat runs in the main window, through `backend.aiRun`.
  - The open note is attached by path, so folder AI rules apply in Rust, and the Agent shows Thinking, Working and Done for it.
  - The Agent's window only shows the panel and posts what was typed, so it still never reads notes or calls a provider.
  - Stopping, closing or replacing a run ignores its late completion.
- The Agent's window is now `side × (side + 40)` for the icon row. With Quick Chat open it grows to at least 340 × (side + 340) (`agentWindowSize`).
  - The main window resizes it through `AgentWindowHost.resize`, newly allowed by `core:window:allow-set-size` on the main window only.
  - `open` now takes a width and height.
- While Quick Chat is open, the Agent stays visible even in "only while busy" mode.
- `AgentView.chat` and the new `chat-*` messages are added to the IPC types.

## Findings

No blocking findings. Notes:

1. The window grows right and down from its top-left corner. Near the bottom or right screen edge the panel can extend off screen. Dragging the Agent up fixes it. Repositioning within the screen's work area can come later.
2. Quick Chat is single-turn: a new question replaces the last. Multi-turn conversations live in the AI block's Grill me skill (T-175).
