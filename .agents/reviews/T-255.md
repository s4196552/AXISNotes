## What it adds

- An **AI chat** button on the ribbon (`core:ai` in `src/features/ribbon/builtin.ts`), after
  Search.
  - It opens AI chat about the open note in the right panel. Pressing it again while the
    chat is showing closes it.
  - It can be shown, hidden and reordered in Settings → Appearance → Ribbon like the other
    buttons.
  - In vaults with a saved order it comes after the saved buttons, as new buttons do.
